# Visual Identity: The Earthly Palette

← [[Home]] · [[Brand Overview]]

Emphasises nature, permanence, and the local, handcrafted quality of the products. HEX codes below are expert-recommended matches to the conceptual colours defined in brand strategy — they are **not** sourced from a formal logo file, and should be treated as the implementation standard going forward.

## Colour Palette

| Role | Colour | HEX | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary (Nature & Trust) | Deep Forest Green | `#154734` | Reflects the garden environment; conveys high quality and enduring strength, aligning with product durability. |
| Secondary (Grit & Material) | Solid Slate Gray | `#404648` | Represents the core material (concrete itself); conveys stability, reliability, permanence. Ideal for structure/backgrounds so colourful ornaments stand out. |
| Accent (Action & Warmth) | Earthy Terracotta | `#CC5500` | Warm contrast for high-visibility CTAs ("Order now via WhatsApp") and contact links; evokes handcrafted, earthen materials. |
| Neutral (Background) | Light Stone White | `#F7F7F7` | Maximum readability and a clean, spacious presentation for the extensive product list; mimics freshly finished stone. |

## Typography

Typography balances the "timeless style" of classical items with the practical need for clear, direct communication (e.g. ordering via phone/WhatsApp). Both are WordPress-friendly Google Fonts.

### Headline Font — Grandeur & Authority
- **Typeface:** Playfair Display (traditional serif)
- **Use:** H1 / H2, main titles, section headers
- **Effect:** elegance and gravitas; grounds the brand in traditional quality

### Body Font — Clarity & Approachability
- **Typeface:** Open Sans (modern sans-serif)
- **Use:** paragraph text, product descriptions, navigation links, CTAs
- **Effect:** extremely legible; essential for managing the variety and detail across the catalogue
