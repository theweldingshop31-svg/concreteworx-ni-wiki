# Brand Voice: "Grit & Grandeur"

← [[Home]] · [[Brand Overview]]

The voice reflects both the strength of the material ("**Grit**") and the aspirational beauty of the finished product ("**Grandeur**"). Four attributes define it.

## 1. Charming & Authentic (The NI Connection)
Warm, locally proud, relationship-focused. Sounds knowledgeable about gardening but accessible — like the best neighbour who always has the perfect suggestion.

- **Goal:** establish immediate trust and locality; foster a sense of community.
- **Example:** *"Achieving timeless style isn't complicated. It's about placing the right bit of personality right where it belongs."*

## 2. Authoritative & Trustworthy (Durability Focus)
Instils confidence in longevity and value — satisfies the customer's psychological need for investment security.

- **Goal:** emphasise long-term value and high-quality craftsmanship.
- **Keywords:** Durable, Handcrafted, Enduring, Timeless, Solid, Weatherproof, Built to last.

## 3. Evocative & Descriptive (Storytelling)
Every product — however unusual (Piggy in Car, Joker Head) — is framed as a piece of garden narrative. Descriptions move beyond dimensions to focus on the feeling the item generates.

- **Goal:** connect the inanimate object to the customer's emotional life (nostalgia, humour, remembrance).
- **Example (memorial plaque):** *"A permanent, gentle marker of a love that withstands every season. Our Bible Memorial, a tribute in enduring stone."*

## 4. Eclectic & Inclusive (Product Range)
Moves smoothly between high-end classical pieces (Roman Bench, Winnie the Pooh Bird Bath) and deeply personal or niche items (football plaques, vehicles). The brand accepts all tastes without judgement.

- **Goal:** validate the diverse interests of the customer base, from traditionalist to hobbyist.

## Voice Application by Product Type

| Product Type | Voice Application | Psychological Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| Planters / Pots | Focus on growth and prosperity | Nurturing / Hope |
| Benches / Large Pieces | Focus on permanence and focal points | Security / Legacy |
| Memorabilia / Plaques | Focus on identity and sentiment | Belonging / Remembrance |
| Whimsical / Animals | Focus on delight and surprise | Joy / Novelty |
