# SEO Strategy — Established & Approved

← [[Home]] · [[Project Status Overview]]

- **Local intent is the anchor:** every title/meta/description targets "Northern Ireland" / "NI" alongside the product term.
- **Product titles are brand-first:** format `"Concrete Worx [Product] Concrete Garden [Type] Northern Ireland"` — not generic product-first titles.
- **Three-tier keyword strategy per product:**
  1. Primary — product + location
  2. Secondary — category + intent
  3. Long-tail — highly specific, low-competition (e.g. *"meerkat bird bath concrete NI"*)
- **Long-tail dominance is the core ranking lever:** 121 unique, specific products let CWNI rank for hundreds of low-competition searches competitors ignore.
- **Descriptions run ~400 words**, structured H2/features/uses/closing, in [[Brand Voice|Grit & Grandeur voice]], ending on a CTA.
- **WhatsApp ordering references were deliberately removed** from product descriptions — handled by a separate WhatsApp chat plugin instead of embedded text.
